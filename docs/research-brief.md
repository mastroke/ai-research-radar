# AI Research Radar — product brief

Keeping up with AI is overwhelming: dozens of papers and repos a day across
arXiv, Hacker News, GitHub, and Hugging Face. People don't need another raw
feed — they need **synthesized signal**: what's new, what connects, and what
actually matters, delivered where they already are.

**AI Research Radar** is a self-hostable agent that scouts those sources daily,
keeps a memory so it never repeats itself, compiles across 2+ sources into a
short opinionated brief, and delivers it to Telegram. You can also send it a
link and get a focused brief back.

**Who it helps first:** Masoob (we run it ourselves to drive the portfolio and
product ideas). **Then:** AI builders, researchers, founders, and investors who
want a daily edge without drowning.

**Monetization:** open-core. Free MIT CLI for distribution; a paid "Radar Pro"
kit (~$29 on Gumroad) with extra connectors, scheduling presets, the two-way
Telegram control bot, and curated prompt packs; a managed hosted tier later.

**Why now:** the ingestion + memory + synthesis engine already exists internally
(our scout). Productizing is mostly packaging, a clean CLI, model-agnostic
backends, and docs — tractable in weeks, not months.

Keep it honest and genuinely useful. No vanity-metric gimmicks.
